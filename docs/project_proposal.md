Project Proposal: ReUseHub

1. Executive Summary
ReUseHub is a web-based community sharing and item reuse platform developed to reduce waste and promote sustainability within local communities and campus environments. The platform allows individuals to list unwanted or surplus pre-owned items (such as books, electronics, stationery, household goods, and furniture) for donation or reuse, enabling other users to request and claim them easily.

2. Project Background and Rationale
In modern consumer societies and academic communities, vast quantities of reusable items are discarded prematurely. At the same time, many students and community members are in need of affordable or free items. ReUseHub bridges this gap by offering a centralized, transparent, and user-friendly digital marketplace specifically dedicated to item reuse and peer-to-peer sharing.

3. Project Objectives
- Encourage Sustainability: Minimize waste by extending the lifecycle of functional products.
- Peer-to-Peer Interaction: Enable seamless discovery and requesting of items among community members.
- Real-time Notifications: Keep donors and requesters updated on request statuses and availability.
- Administrative Control: Provide moderators with oversight on item listings, user accounts, and community requests.

4. Target Audience
- University and college students and staff looking to exchange books, equipment, and supplies.
- Community members seeking to donate gently used household goods.
- Local community organizations and sustainability groups.

5. Feasibility and Technology Stack
- Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla JS with responsive design)
- Backend: PHP (Procedural / Modular Architecture)
- Database: MySQL / MariaDB (via XAMPP)
- Server: Apache HTTP Server (XAMPP Environment)
